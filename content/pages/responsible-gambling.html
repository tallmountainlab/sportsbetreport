--- title: Responsible gambling description: Free, confidential help for anyone affected by gambling, and the tools that keep betting under control. ---

If gambling is causing you or someone you love stress, help is free and confidential. Call or text 1-800-GAMBLER (1-800-426-2537), 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Where to get help

  • National Council on Problem Gambling: the national helpline, chat and local treatment finder.
  • Gamblers Anonymous: free peer support meetings, in person and online.
  • Gam-Anon: support for family members and friends.
  • In a crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline).

Tools that help

  • Limits and self-exclusion: licensed sportsbooks must offer deposit limits, time limits, cool-off periods and self-exclusion. Many states also run a statewide self-exclusion list.
  • Blocking software: apps that block gambling sites and apps on your phone and computer.
  • Bank blocks: many banks and card issuers let you block gambling transactions from their app.
