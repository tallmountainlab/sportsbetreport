How we make money

The daily report is free to read. It may be supported by:

  • Sponsors of the website or the email newsletter, always labeled "Sponsor".
  • Partner links to betting tools, data services and stat sites, labeled "Partner". If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
  • Display advertising.

Sponsors and partners never change the numbers. The lines in every report come straight from the market data.

What we won't do: we don't accept money from sportsbooks to link to them, we don't sell picks, and we don't run ads aimed at people under 21.
