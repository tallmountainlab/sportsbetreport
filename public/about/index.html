About the Sportsbet Report

Some of the best mornings started the same way: the paper hitting the porch, a cup of coffee, and the sports section spread across the kitchen table. Box scores, standings, and that little column of lines everybody pretended not to read closely.

The Sportsbet Report is our way of bringing that morning back. Every day before the first game, we put out one clean page: the spreads, totals and moneylines for the NFL, college football, NBA, NHL and MLB, which numbers moved overnight, and what they say about each team's chances. No noise, no hype, no hot takes.

The numbers come straight from the market. We use The Odds API, which collects the major US sportsbooks, or ESPN's public scoreboard when that feed is down. We take the middle of the books so one oddball number doesn't throw things off.

What we're not

  • We're not a sportsbook. We don't take bets or hold anybody's money.
  • We don't sell picks, and we don't link to sportsbooks.
  • Nothing here tells you to bet. Sports betting is legal in some states and illegal in others, including Utah. Know the rules where you live.
